Finding us

Kinlochard is approximately 30 miles north of Glasgow city centre and 20 miles west of Stirling. Note that mobile coverage is poor beyond Aberfoyle, so it's worth saving these directions before you set off.

Directions from Aberfoyle
  1. 01Drive through the village of Aberfoyle and follow the B829 west.
  2. 02After 4 miles you'll pass Forest Hills Hotel on your right, and shortly after you'll enter the village of Kinlochard.
  3. 03Turn left at the junction shortly after the Kinlochard village sign, passing the red telephone box.
  4. 04The hall is 100m along this road on the right.
Parking

Please keep the parking bay at the hall free, and don't park on the road directly outside the venue. If you're staying at Forest Hills you're welcome to leave your car at the hotel and walk or share a lift across.

If you're coming for the day, parking at the layby next to the village hall is available but the spaces are limited. The Forestry car park at the end of the tarmac road has 8–10 spaces and is a 5–10 minute walk from the hall (map), and there's a layby on the B829 opposite Ledard Farm.
